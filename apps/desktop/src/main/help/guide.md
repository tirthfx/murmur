# Murmur guide

Murmur turns speech into text in whatever app you are using. Speech recognition and text
cleanup run on this computer.

## Dictate

1. Click where the text should go.
2. Hold the dictation key, speak, and let go.
3. The text appears. The small Bar at the bottom of the screen shows each step.

Tap the key twice to keep recording hands-free, and press it once more to stop. Escape
cancels, also while the text is being worked on. Typing another key while holding the
dictation key cancels too.

## Shortcuts

Open Settings, then Shortcuts, to see and change every shortcut. There are shortcuts for
copying and pasting the last transcript, pressing Enter, rewriting selected text, opening the
Scratchpad and recording a meeting.

## The Bar

Click it to start hands-free dictation. Right-click it to choose the microphone, language or
cleanup level for the next dictation. Drag it anywhere; near a side edge it docks.

## Better text

- Cleanup level (Settings, System) decides how much is tidied: fillers, corrections and
  lists.
- Smart Formatting writes numbers, times, money and addresses the usual way.
- Add names and terms to the Dictionary, and text you say often to Snippets.
- Say "number one ... number two" or "bullet ... bullet" for a list, and "press enter" at the
  end to press Return.

## Rewrite and commands

Select text and use the Rewrite shortcut to change it. Command Mode and Instruct Mode are
described on their own pages in Settings.

## Meetings

The Notetaker page records a meeting, writes the transcript, summary and tasks, and lets you
ask questions about past meetings. Type "@" in notes to mention a contact. Tell the other
people that you are recording.

## Your data

Data and Privacy shows what is kept and for how long, and lets you export or delete it.
